I am an automotive engineer with a Bachelor of Engineering in Automotive Engineering from NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi (2025). I currently work as Product Manager, Localization at Lucky Motor Corporation (Kia Pakistan), where I lead component localization across the Kia Sorento, Sportage and Stonic platforms. I coordinate engineering, R&D, quality, procurement and supplier teams to turn product requirements into locally manufacturable components.

My research centers on vehicle energy efficiency. For my final year project I designed and prototyped a regenerative anti-lock braking system with regenerative suspension, combining regenerative braking, electromagnetic suspension and thermoelectric energy harvesting. The project received Ignite Final Year Project funding, and I am a co-author of an EngrXiv preprint on hybrid vehicle energy recovery. I also work with Python, SQL and machine learning to analyze supplier performance, and I am co-developing a predictive model for vendor performance and delivery risk.

I am seeking a PhD or research-based Master's program at the intersection of automotive systems, energy recovery, and intelligent, data-driven engineering. Download my CV (PDF).
